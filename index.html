How to use this website

Eight simple steps from login to a downloaded PDF.

1

Login

Enter your full name and email address on the Login page, then select Login. No password is needed.

2

Select a resume template

Open the Templates page and pick the design that fits the role you're applying for.

3

Fill in your CV details

Add your personal, education, project and skill information into the guided form.

4

Upload a profile photo

Choose a photo from your device, or use your camera on a phone that supports it.

5

Generate your CV

Select Generate CV once every required field is filled in.

6

Preview your CV

Your finished resume opens on its own page, laid out in the template you chose.

7

Edit your CV

Select Edit CV at any time to return to the form with everything already filled in.

8

Save and download

Select Download CV as PDF. Your phone or computer saves it through its normal download system, so you'll find it in Downloads or your Files app.
